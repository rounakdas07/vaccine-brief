# TG4050 · Phase 1 — comparison with VISTA

Vaccine Brief | Rounak Das | Evidence reviewed 2026-11-18
Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with Infinitopes.

## Reported evidence
TG4050 | Phase 1 | Journal publication
Resected HPV-negative head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
Relapses in the immediate-vaccine per-protocol group: 0 / 16
Control group: 3 / 16
Population: Per-protocol analysis · not all 33 randomised patients
Follow-up: 30 months median; no additional relapses at 41-month update
The reported per-protocol analysis found no relapses in the immediate-vaccine group.

## Before quoting this result
One early relapse was excluded from that denominator.
A vaccinated patient who relapsed after two doses did not meet the minimum-exposure rule. “No vaccinated patient relapsed” would misstate this result.

## Interpretation for a VISTA discussion (author appraisal)
A relevant viral-vector precedent, but individualised targets and a postoperative setting differ from ITOP1. The small study cannot establish comparative efficacy.
An encouraging early signal whose per-protocol denominator must travel with the headline.

## Design differences
Patients
  TG4050 · Phase 1: Resected HPV-negative head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
  VISTA: Resectable oesophageal / gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma

Vaccine approach
  TG4050 · Phase 1: Individualised antigens · MVA viral vector
  VISTA: Shared antigens · viral vector

Timing
  TG4050 · Phase 1: After surgery and adjuvant treatment
  VISTA: Prime before surgery; boost after surgery

Treatment comparison
  TG4050 · Phase 1: Immediate vaccination vs observation with vaccination offered at relapse
  VISTA: Alongside perioperative standard care; saline control in Phase 2a

Study design
  TG4050 · Phase 1: 33 randomised (17 vs 16); 32 in the reported per-protocol analysis
  VISTA: 8-person safety lead-in, then 52 planned in a 3:1 randomised comparison

Main evidence shown
  TG4050 · Phase 1: Early safety / feasibility; exploratory disease-free survival
  VISTA: Primary: safety and tolerability. Immunogenicity and preliminary efficacy are secondary.

# INTerpath-001 — comparison with VISTA

Vaccine Brief | Rounak Das | Evidence reviewed 2026-11-18
Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with Infinitopes.

## Reported evidence
Intismeran autogene · V940 | Phase 3 | Sponsor report
Completely resected stage IIB–IV melanoma
RFS hazard ratio: 0.58
95% CI 0.45–0.75
Population: 1,137 patients · intention-to-treat
Follow-up: 24.6 months median follow-up
Moderna reports a lower hazard of recurrence or death with vaccine + pembrolizumab versus the control regimen.

## Before quoting this result
42% is a relative hazard reduction.
It is not a 42-percentage-point absolute benefit. This brief uses the ITT estimate; the sponsor separately reports a more favourable modified-ITT estimate.

## Interpretation for a VISTA discussion (author appraisal)
Useful evidence for the cancer-vaccine field. It does not establish ITOP1 efficacy or show that shared antigens outperform individualised vaccines.
A positive randomised melanoma result is a field precedent, not a transferable effect size for ITOP1.

# KEYNOTE-942 — comparison with VISTA

Vaccine Brief | Rounak Das | Evidence reviewed 2026-11-18
Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with Infinitopes.

## Reported evidence
Intismeran autogene · V940 | Phase 2b | Journal publication
Resected high-risk stage III/IV melanoma
RFS hazard ratio: 0.510
95% CI 0.294–0.887
Population: 157 randomised · 107 combination / 50 control
Follow-up: 60.3 months median planned follow-up
The five-year update reports continued separation in RFS for the combination. The analyses were descriptive.

## Before quoting this result
Long follow-up does not make every endpoint conclusive.
The overall-survival estimate had a confidence interval spanning 1. Do not convert a recurrence finding into a proven survival benefit.

## Interpretation for a VISTA discussion (author appraisal)
A useful durability reference for discussions about follow-up. Its population, platform and pembrolizumab backbone differ from VISTA.
The five-year melanoma update informs durability discussions, with descriptive analyses and uncertain overall survival.

# Autogene cevumeran — comparison with VISTA

Vaccine Brief | Rounak Das | Evidence reviewed 2026-11-18
Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with Infinitopes.

## Reported evidence
Autogene cevumeran · BNT122 | Phase 1 | Journal publication
Resected pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
Vaccinated patients with induced T-cell responses: 8 / 16
Responders vs non-responders: 8 vs 8
Population: 16 vaccinated · no randomised unvaccinated comparator
Follow-up: 3.2 years median follow-up
Vaccine-induced T cells persisted, and immune responders had longer RFS than non-responders.

## Before quoting this result
Immune response was not randomly assigned.
The responder comparison is an association within a treated cohort. It cannot isolate the vaccine’s effect from patient differences or other treatments.

## Interpretation for a VISTA discussion (author appraisal)
Supports investigating durable immune responses in a gastrointestinal cancer. It does not supply a causal recurrence-benefit estimate for ITOP1.
Durable immune responses are a biological signal; the responder analysis is not a randomised efficacy comparison.

# MAGRIT — comparison with VISTA

Vaccine Brief | Rounak Das | Evidence reviewed 2026-11-18
Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with Infinitopes.

## Reported evidence
MAGE-A3 immunotherapeutic | Phase 3 | Journal publication
Resected stage IB–IIIA MAGE-A3-positive non-small-cell lung cancer
DFS hazard ratio: 1.02
95% CI 0.89–1.18 · p = 0.74
Population: 2,312 randomised · 2,272 treated
Follow-up: Historical Phase 3 report · 2016
The trial did not demonstrate a disease-free-survival benefit.

## Before quoting this result
A negative programme is not a verdict on every shared-antigen vaccine.
Its antigen, delivery platform, cancer and treatment era differ from ITOP1. Keep it in the evidence set without generalising its outcome to the entire class.

## Interpretation for a VISTA discussion (author appraisal)
A useful counterweight to positive-only narratives. ITOP1 still needs its own clinical evidence, even with a distinct antigen-discovery and vector strategy.
Include negative precedent and explain the biological differences; do not infer a class-wide effect.

Patients
  MAGRIT: Resected stage IB–IIIA MAGE-A3-positive non-small-cell lung cancer
  VISTA: Resectable oesophageal / gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma

Vaccine approach
  MAGRIT: Shared antigen · recombinant protein immunotherapeutic
  VISTA: Shared antigens · viral vector

Timing
  MAGRIT: After surgery
  VISTA: Prime before surgery; boost after surgery

Treatment comparison
  MAGRIT: MAGE-A3 immunotherapeutic vs placebo; with or without prior adjuvant chemotherapy
  VISTA: Alongside perioperative standard care; saline control in Phase 2a

Study design
  MAGRIT: Randomised, double-blind; 2,312 assigned, 2,272 received treatment
  VISTA: 8-person safety lead-in, then 52 planned in a 3:1 randomised comparison

Main evidence shown
  MAGRIT: Disease-free survival (DFS)
  VISTA: Primary: safety and tolerability. Immunogenicity and preliminary efficacy are secondary.
