# Rounak's walkthrough

## One sentence

I built Vaccine Brief to turn cancer-vaccine trial results into source-linked competitor briefs, keeping the important caveats attached.

## A 45-second demonstration

1. Open the tool. TG4050 is selected.
2. Point to the reported `0 / 16` relapses. It is explicitly a per-protocol population.
3. Read the adjacent caveat: an early relapse after two doses was excluded from that population. The tool helps prevent an overbroad claim about all vaccinated patients.
4. Open the design comparison. VISTA differs in cancer type, antigen strategy and vaccination timing.
5. Download the brief. Sources, limitations and a question for the team are included.

## What you can honestly say

“I researched the public evidence and built this with AI assistance. The value I was aiming for is less repeated extraction and clearer clinical comparisons. I would like your team's feedback on whether this format is useful.”

Do not claim the company commissioned it, that you discovered an unknown scientific result, that it is clinically validated, that it proves ITOP1 efficacy or that you measured time saved. The underlying findings belong to the researchers. This is an evidence-organisation and appraisal prototype.

## Questions you should be able to answer

**Why Infinitopes?** Its clinical programme gives a specific reference against which to organise other cancer-vaccine results. Your oncology literature-review experience is relevant to maintaining the evidence.

**Why not rank vaccines?** The trials have different cancers, regimens, endpoints, follow-up and analysis populations. A raw ranking would imply a comparison they did not test.

**What is intention-to-treat?** Analysing participants in the groups they were randomised to. It helps preserve the randomised comparison. Per-protocol and modified analyses answer different questions and can introduce selection bias.

**What does a hazard ratio of 0.58 mean?** The analysis estimates a relative hazard of 0.58 for the endpoint in that trial. It is not a 42-percentage-point absolute reduction and is not a prediction for another vaccine.

**What does no VISTA outcome data mean?** We did not identify reported clinical outcomes in the reviewed sources. It does not mean the trial failed, and the planned sample size is not a completed cohort.

**Where could you help?** Source verification, clinical-evidence extraction, literature surveillance and maintaining reviewed briefs. These are plausible tasks to discuss, not a confirmed vacancy or promise of remote work.
