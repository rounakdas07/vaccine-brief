## Inferred useful contribution

A clinical-stage team may need to explain how its programme fits emerging clinical evidence during partnering, strategy and investor discussions. A compact source-linked comparison could reduce repeated extraction and help preserve important qualifications when a result is reused.

That is a value hypothesis, not a discovered internal failure. We have not seen Infinitopes' systems, spoken to its team, measured time saved or confirmed demand. The tool should be offered as a concrete example of evidence-review work, not as a claim to have solved its R&D or commercial strategy.

## Why this scope

The chosen set contains five human studies in resected solid tumours: INTerpath-001, KEYNOTE-942, TG4050, a pancreatic autogene-cevumeran Phase 1 cohort, and MAGRIT. VISTA is held constant as a reference. Selection was purposive: contrasting evidence maturity, platforms, analysis populations and positive/negative results. This introduces selection bias; the set is not exhaustive.

The practical output is a plain-text brief suitable for review, not another predictive model. No private data, tumour sequencing, patient records or experimental efficacy model are required.

## Findings that shaped the product

- An intention-to-treat result and a modified-ITT result should not be interchanged.
- A per-protocol efficacy population can omit an early relapse; the exclusion needs to travel with the headline.
- Comparing immune responders and non-responders in a treated cohort is not equivalent to randomising a vaccine.
- A recurrence endpoint is not overall survival.
- Negative historical results belong in the set, with their era and platform differences visible.
- Registry text and sponsor announcements can differ. The VISTA regimen and recruitment descriptions are not silently reconciled.

The complete supporting references, access notes and result details live in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) and the structured dataset. Every study has a specific source locator. Direct publisher access was unavailable for some papers; the tool explicitly discloses use of indexed primary-paper sections or abstracts. It does not claim an exhaustive full-text critical appraisal.

## Design decisions

A restrained editorial layout uses one selected trial, one reported result and one caveat at a time. Study-design details and provenance expand below. Mobile uses a native study selector. Exports preserve the caveats; the product does not offer rankings or a misleading cross-trial chart.

## What would validate the value

Ask a clinical or business-development reviewer to prepare a short comparison with and without this tool. Record time to the correct source, extraction errors, omitted caveats and whether the generated brief survives review. Agree the next studies and preferred format with that reviewer before expanding the dataset.
