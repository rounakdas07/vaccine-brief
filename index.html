Scope & method

A brief you can check.

An independent research tool by Rounak Das, medical student at the University of Nottingham.

What is included

Five selected human studies in resected solid tumours, plus VISTA as the reference. Positive, early and negative findings are included. This is a focused starting set, not a systematic review or complete competitive landscape.

How to use it

Read the finding and caveat together. Open the original source before quoting externally. Downloaded briefs retain the limitations, reference-study details and source URLs.

Limits

No pooled estimates, cross-trial efficacy ranking or predicted ITOP1 benefit. Source access varies and is disclosed per study. This snapshot does not update automatically; clinical review is needed before professional use.
