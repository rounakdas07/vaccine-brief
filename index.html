What is included

Five selected human studies in resected solid tumours, plus VISTA as the reference. The set includes positive, early and negative findings. It is a focused starting set, not a systematic review or complete competitive landscape.

How to use it

Read the finding and its caveat together. Open the original source before quoting externally. Downloaded briefs retain the limitations, reference-study details and source URLs.

What stays uncertain

No pooled estimates, cross-trial efficacy ranking or predicted ITOP1 benefit. Source access varies and is disclosed per study. This snapshot does not update automatically; clinical review is needed before professional use.
